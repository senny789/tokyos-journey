# TJ-05 · Find toys and bring them to Senny

**Status** — Proposed

Tokyo finds a toy, carries it to Senny, and hands it over.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tokyo can pick up at least one toy and the player can tell which toy she is carrying.
- [ ] Interacting with Senny while carrying a toy hands that toy to Senny.
- [ ] After the handoff, Senny has the toy and Tokyo's carried-toy display updates.
