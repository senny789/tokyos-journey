# TJ-10 · Earn clothes and toys by completing levels

**Status** — Proposed

Tokyo earns clothes and new toys as rewards for finishing levels.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Level completion shows which clothes or toys Tokyo earned.
- [ ] At least one level awards clothing and at least one level awards a toy.
- [ ] Earned rewards remain available after the player continues to another level.
- [ ] Replaying a completed level cannot remove an unlocked item or accidentally grant a second copy of a one-time unlock.

## Open question

Which items should each level award, and how should Tokyo wear or use them?
