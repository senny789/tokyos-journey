# TJ-12 · Increase Tokyo's level and stamina

**Status** — Proposed

Tokyo increases her character level and maximum stamina as she progresses through the journey.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The player can see Tokyo's character level and her current and maximum stamina.
- [ ] Reaching a defined progression milestone raises Tokyo's character level and increases her maximum stamina according to the progression rules.
- [ ] The game shows the level-up and carries the new values into later levels.

## Open question

What earns a character level, how much stamina does each level add, and which actions spend stamina?
