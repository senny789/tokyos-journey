# TJ-09 · Grow from puppy to adult

**Status** — Proposed

Tokyo starts the journey as a puppy and grows into an adult as the story progresses.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tokyo appears as a puppy at the beginning of the journey.
- [ ] An agreed story milestone shows Tokyo growing up and updates her appearance in subsequent scenes.
