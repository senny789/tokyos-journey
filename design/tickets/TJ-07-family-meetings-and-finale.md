# TJ-07 · Meet the family and reunite in the finale

**Status** — Proposed

Tokyo meets her family members one by one along the journey. In the finale, she sees the whole family together.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each family member on the agreed roster has an individual encounter before the finale.
- [ ] Each encounter introduces the family member so the player can recognize them later.
