# Level 2 — The kennel

## Story

Tokyo is a tiny puppy again, back in the kennel where she was born and raised. Dogs were bred and kept in cages here. Tokyo needs to escape, and she finds Sandesh at the end.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let Tokyo squeeze through small gaps between cages. Build the route around how small she is.

## Implemented playthrough

Tokyo appears as a smaller puppy in a cage-filled kennel. Walk to the wall latch and press F to open the front gate, then cross the room to find Sandesh and use the far portal.
