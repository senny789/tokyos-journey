# Level 3 — The first house

## Story

Tokyo is a little bigger than she was at the kennel. She has to find the exit while the dark entity roams the house. Patches of light protect her. If it catches her, it’s game over. She finds Sushil after escaping.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let the player hear the entity through walls and choose whether to hide or run for a nearby light.
- Show the entity stopping at the edge of a lit patch before it returns to its patrol.

## Implemented playthrough

Tokyo searches a darkened house for the exit while the roaming shadow closes in between light patches. Interact with Sushil at the far end, then use the exit portal. The patch shield, retry panel, and home option make the chase rules clear.
