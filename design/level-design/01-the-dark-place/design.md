# Level 1 — The dark place

## Story

After falling asleep in [Level 0 — Home before the dream](../00-home-before-the-dream/design.md), Tokyo wakes up in a dark, unfamiliar place. She sees a dim light and hears Senny’s voice from far away. She follows the voice to a rift and steps through to the kennel.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let the player use the movement they learned at home to jump across gaps and follow Senny’s voice.

## Implemented playthrough

Tokyo crosses three connected sections while the camera follows her. The Ledges follow the sketch, The Descent takes her down into the gap and back up, and The Far Side has wider gaps and taller jumps. In The Far Side, climb to the highest rock and hold a leftward jump to reach the glowing light. Landing beside the light collects it and opens Senny’s rift. Interact at the open rift to enter the kennel. A fall in any section brings Tokyo back to the starting ledge and returns the light to its platform.
