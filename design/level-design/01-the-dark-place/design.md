# Level 1 — The dark place

## Story

After falling asleep in [Level 0 — Home before the dream](../00-home-before-the-dream/design.md), Tokyo wakes up in a dark, unfamiliar place. She sees a dim light and hears Senny’s voice from far away. She follows the voice to a light portal and steps through to the kennel.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let the player use the movement they learned at home to follow the light and listen for Senny’s voice.

## Implemented playthrough

Tokyo crosses a nearly black landscape toward Senny’s distant voice and a glowing portal at the far right. Warm light patches form safe islands around a roaming shadow. Run between the patches, interact at the portal, and the dream shifts into the kennel. A caught Tokyo can retry the stage or wake at home.
