# Level 8 — The toy world

## Story

Tokyo enters a fun place full of toys. At the end she finds her favorite plushie.

## Gameplay ideas

- Give Tokyo time to play and explore. She could sniff out her own plushie among the other toys.

## Implemented playthrough

Explore a bright toy-filled room, find the bunny plushie, and press F to pick it up. Tokyo carries it beside her for the last two dream stages. The exit only opens after she has recovered her favorite toy.
