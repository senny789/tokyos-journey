# Level 6 — The city

## Story

Tokyo navigates the city and encounters cats and other dogs along the way. She finds Reshika at the end.

## Gameplay ideas

- Give Tokyo multiple routes through the city. A friendly cat could lead her to a shortcut.

## Implemented playthrough

Meet both city cats, Mikan and Sora, with F; then find Reshika. One stray dog patrols the route and briefly slows Tokyo if it brushes her. The firefly lights make safe places to regroup. The exit unlocks after both cats and Reshika have been found.
