# Level 7 — The puzzle house

## Story

Tokyo has to solve puzzles to find her way through a house. At the end she finally meets Senny, whose voice she heard at the start of the dream.

## Gameplay ideas

- Build puzzles around combining movement, scent, and sound clues.

## Implemented playthrough

The familiar living room becomes a dream puzzle. Press the moon stone, then the star stone; the order is repeated in the prompt. Meet Senny after solving the sequence, then use the portal. Pressing the star too early resets the stones.
