# Level 4 — The dark forest

## Story

Tokyo has to find her way through a dark forest while avoiding wild animals and other creatures. She meets Dad at the end.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let Tokyo listen for nearby creatures before crossing exposed ground.

## Implemented playthrough

Follow the firefly lights through the moonlit forest. Jump over the patrolling owl. If it brushes Tokyo, she pauses briefly before continuing. Meet Dad near the right-hand portal, interact, then enter the portal to continue.
