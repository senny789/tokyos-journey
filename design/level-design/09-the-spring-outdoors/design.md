# Level 9 — The spring outdoors

## Story

Tokyo reaches a cheerful outdoor area full of spring flowers and cherry blossom trees. She meets the whole family at the end. Then she wakes up at home and plays with everyone, ending the game.

## Gameplay ideas

- Let the player run up to the family in the dream. After Tokyo wakes up, let the player choose whom to greet or run to her plushie.

## Implemented playthrough

Run through the blossomed spring garden to the whole family and interact for a reunion. After the cheering beat, follow the morning cue and press F near the right edge to wake Tokyo. The final home scene lets the player greet the family again, play fetch with Senny, and choose Dream Again.
