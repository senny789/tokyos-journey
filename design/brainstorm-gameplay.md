# Tokyo’s Journey — gameplay brainstorming

## Core play style

The suggested direction is a 2D adventure with light platforming, puzzles, exploration, and stealth. Tokyo uses movement, scent, and sound to find her way. Keep her basic controls familiar across stages, then change what the player needs to notice or do in each one. Early stages introduce skills one at a time. Later stages give the player room to combine them.

- **Scent.** At a fork, Tokyo could stop and sniff to catch a brief trace of a family member’s scent. The scent gives a direction. The player still works out how to get there. In the toy world, Tokyo could use it to pick out her own plushie.
- **Sound.** Senny’s distant voice can teach the player to listen for direction. Later, the player could listen for the entity or wild animals before crossing a room or clearing.
- **Movement.** As a puppy, Tokyo could squeeze through gaps in the kennel. As the journey continues, the surroundings give her familiar moves different uses.
- **Exploration.** Landmarks, hidden routes, and encounters can reward curiosity. A friendly cat in the city could lead to a shortcut.

## Story and progression

The opening establishes Tokyo’s home, her family, and her favorite plushie. She plays, eats her treats, and falls asleep. The dream takes her through nine stages before she wakes up and plays with everyone again.

| Dream stage | Story beat | Gameplay suggestion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The dark place | Tokyo follows Senny’s distant voice to a dimly lit portal. | Teach movement and listening by letting the player follow the voice and the light. |
| 2. The kennel | Tokyo is a tiny puppy again. She escapes the kennel and finds Sandesh. | Squeeze through small gaps and find a route past the cages. Her size makes the escape feel vulnerable. |
| 3. The first house | Tokyo is a little bigger. The entity hunts her, and she finds Sushil after escaping. | Explore rooms, listen for footsteps, hide under furniture, and plan routes between lit patches. |
| 4. The dark forest | Tokyo avoids wild creatures and reaches Dad. | Listen for nearby animals before crossing exposed ground. |
| 5. The grassland | Tokyo avoids hostile dogs in daylight and finds Mom. | Use the longer sightlines to spot dogs and choose a route across the open grass. |
| 6. The city | Tokyo encounters cats and other dogs on her way to Reshika. | Find routes through the city and optionally follow a friendly cat to a shortcut. |
| 7. The puzzle house | Tokyo solves puzzles and finally finds Senny. | Combine movement, scent, and sound clues in puzzles with time to think. |
| 8. The toy world | Tokyo finds her favorite plushie in a playful world full of toys. | Let the player slow down and play, then use scent to find the plushie. |
| 9. The spring outdoors | Tokyo runs through flowers and cherry blossoms to the whole family. | Let the player control Tokyo as she runs to the family. After she wakes up, give the player control again to choose whom to greet or run to the plushie. |

## The entity and safe light

The entity roams the dream levels and can surprise Tokyo as she explores. Getting caught means game over. Patches of light protect her.

- Give the player a cue before the entity appears, such as slow footsteps on the other side of a wall.
- If the entity spots Tokyo, she could break line of sight and hide. It could search nearby before moving on.
- When Tokyo reaches a lit patch, show the entity stopping at its edge. It could linger briefly before returning to its patrol so the player can listen for it to move away.
- Let the player choose whether to slip into a hiding spot or run for a nearby light. Remembering the lights makes it easier to explore confidently.

**Open decision.** Should lit patches also be checkpoints, or should a game over send Tokyo back to the start of the current stage?

## Earlier ideas to draw from

- **Emotion as a rule.** A hallway that loops until Tokyo notices a changing detail could express confusion. A shadow tracing a hidden path could express fear turning into understanding. Floating scenery Tokyo can ride could express wonder.
- **Learning to find her own way.** A later area could bring back an earlier landmark or dream rule so the player recognizes how to get through it.
- **Other progression ideas.** Explore hidden routes and open areas through relationships, learn moves that make familiar areas traversable in new ways, or follow clues and make choices about whom to trust.
